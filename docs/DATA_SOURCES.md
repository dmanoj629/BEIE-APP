# BEIE Data Sources & Provenance Registry

Every observation presented in BEIE is tied to an auditable public record.

| Dataset Identifier | Publisher / Source | Series & Frequency | Access Method & License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cpi_all_india_2026` | Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) | All-India CPI (Base 2024=100), Monthly (Jan 2025 – Sep 2026) | Official MoSPI Press Release / OGDL India |
| `cpi_karnataka_2026` | MoSPI | Karnataka State CPI & Division Indices, Monthly | Annexure III & eSankhyiki Portal / OGDL India |
| `crude_basket_ppac` | Petroleum Planning & Analysis Cell (PPAC), MoPNG | Indian Crude Basket Price ($/bbl & ₹/bbl), Monthly | PPAC Price Releases / Fair Use & Public Disclosure |
| `fuel_retail_delhi_blr` | PPAC / IOCL Public Notices | Retail Selling Price of Petrol & Diesel, Major Cities | PPAC Daily & Monthly Archives |
| `io_matrix_india_15` | CSO / MoSPI / Chadha, Saluja & Sivamani aggregated | 15-Sector Domestic Absorption Matrix ($A$) | Aggregated from National Accounts SUT / Public Academic Release |
| `rbi_repo_cpi_elasticity` | Reserve Bank of India (RBI) | RBI Bulletin July 2025 Study & Monetary Policy Reports | RBI Publications / Open Academic Reference |
| `mandi_agmarknet_crops` | Directorate of Marketing & Inspection / data.gov.in | Karnataka Mandi Wholesale Prices (Rice, Maize, Pulses) | AGMARKNET API / data.gov.in (OGDL) |
