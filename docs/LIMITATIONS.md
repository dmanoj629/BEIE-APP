# BEIE Honest Limitations & Boundary Declarations

As required by the BEIE Blueprint:
1. **Scenario-Based, Not Point Forecasts**:
   BEIE models structural transmission conditional on explicit scenarios and assumptions. It does not claim to know the future or provide guaranteed point forecasts.
2. **Food Price Dynamics in 2026**:
   Between March and September 2026, headline CPI inflation rose from 3.40% to 5.54%. Crucially, September food inflation reached 7.74% (with specific spikes like vegetable inflation). Global crude oil accounts for only part of this headline movement; domestic supply disruptions and climate anomalies cannot be attributed to oil alone.
3. **State Tax Disparities**:
   Retail fuel prices in Karnataka differ from Delhi due to state VAT, cess, and local freight margins. When national proxy pump prices are referenced, they are explicitly tagged as proxies.
4. **No Regulated Financial Advice**:
   BEIE strictly avoids personal stock picks, price predictions, or regulated investment advice. All financial calculators, retail loss statistics, and asset screeners are educational.
5. **No Silent Synthetic Fallbacks**:
   When real data is unavailable (e.g., inaccessible PDS portal or missing seasonal series), the system does not inject mock numbers; it returns mode `limited` and explicitly states the blocker.
